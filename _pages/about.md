I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Utah State University](https://www.usu.edu/), 
working on **Computer Vision** and **Vision-Language Models (VLMs)**. My research focuses 
on biological video understanding, dataset development, and multimodal learning 
for embryo viability assessment. I completed my M.S. in Computer Science at Utah State 
University in 2026 and my B.Sc. in Computer Science at 
[Azarbaijan Shahid Madani University](https://intl.azaruniv.ac.ir/) in 2023, where I ranked 
**1st** in my cohort.
