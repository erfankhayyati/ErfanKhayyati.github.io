We introduce **BovEmbryo**, the first publicly available full-development time-lapse video 
dataset and benchmark for bovine embryo viability assessment, containing 344 annotated 
seven-day IVF and SCNT videos with six-class origin–outcome labels. We define three 
benchmark tasks — joint six-class classification, three-class outcome-only classification, 
and embryo-origin generalization — evaluated across five video architectures, finding that 
outcome-only classification (best: 88.96% accuracy) is substantially more tractable than 
joint classification (best: 79.06%), while cross-origin generalization remains an open 
challenge. Dataset, annotations, and code are released on Hugging Face and GitHub under 
CC BY-NC 4.0.
